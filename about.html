About the Library Hub

A calm, well-equipped place on campus to read, research and work together.

Our library

The Library Hub is the Central Library on the Main Campus. It brings silent reading, group study, research and discussion rooms under one roof, and it lets you reserve a seat online before you arrive.

The library is open to every student, faculty member and guest.

Mission

To give every member of the campus a comfortable place to learn, with the space, resources and help they need.

Vision

A library that is easy to use, easy to reach and always ready for the next student who walks in.

Library rules

  • Arrive within 15 minutes or your seat is released.
  • Silent zone: no calls or group talk.
  • Carry your ID card for the desk check.
  • Keep drinks in closed bottles only.

Need help? Visit the help desk on the 1st floor.

Facilities

Silent study zone

12 seats, ground floor.

Group study zone

12 seats, first floor.

Research zone

Desks and partitions beside reference books.

Discussion rooms

Two rooms for 6 and 4 people, second floor.

Computer desks & lounge

Computers by the windows and armchairs to relax.

Projector & laptop lockers

Projector at the help desk. 8 lockers on the ground floor.

Why students should use the library

A seat when you need it

Book ahead, so you do not spend your study time looking for a place.

The right space for the task

Choose silence for revision, a group table for projects or a room for discussion.

Free for students

Student membership and seat booking cost nothing.

Long hours

Open 12 hours on weekdays and longer in exam weeks.
