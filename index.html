Welcome to the Library Hub

Our reading rooms are open to every student, faculty member and guest. Reserve a place in advance so you spend less time searching and more time learning.

I have always imagined that Paradise will be a kind of library.

How to book

  1. Choose a date and time slot.
  2. Pick a free seat on the seat map.
  3. Fill in your details.
  4. Press Book now and show your booking ID at the desk.

Opening hours

  • Monday – Friday: 08:00 – 20:00
  • Saturday: 09:00 – 17:00
  • Sunday: closed
  • Exam weeks: 07:00 – 23:00
